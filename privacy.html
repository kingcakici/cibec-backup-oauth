Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

About this application

CIBEC Backup Drive is a privately operated tool used by its authorized operator for Google Drive backup and recovery operations.

Google Drive access

The application requests access to Google Drive only with the account holder's authorization. This access is used to upload, download, list, and manage backup files.

Data storage and sharing

Backup content is stored in the Google Drive account authorized by the operator. This application is not intended to sell personal information or provide access to unauthorized third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Backup retention and deletion are controlled by the authorized operator through Google Drive and the backup configuration.

Security

The operator uses access controls and may use encryption to protect backup files. This public website does not contain backup files or secrets.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
