CIBEC Backup Drive

CIBEC Backup Drive is a privately operated backup integration used to securely transfer and restore authorized backup files through Google Drive.

Purpose

The application uses OAuth 2.0 to access Google Drive with the account holder's authorization. It is used for backup storage and recovery operations, not as a public consumer service.

Data handling

Access is limited to accounts explicitly authorized by the operator. Backup files may contain operational and personal information and are handled under restricted access controls.

Privacy

Read our Privacy Policy.

This website does not provide access to backup files, passwords, or system administration.
